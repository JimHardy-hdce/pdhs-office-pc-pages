PRIVATE ARCHIVE UTILITY

PDHS office PC

A personal-use utility operated by Jim Hardy to organize selected Palm Desert High School Vocal Music Boosters SMS/MMS conversations from his own SMS Backup & Restore backups.

The utility is designed to retrieve backups from a designated Google Drive folder, process only owner-confirmed conversations on the owner's computer, and save transcripts, summaries and supporting records to an owner-private Google Drive folder. Repeat runs are designed to avoid duplicate records.

This website provides application information only. It does not host backups, messages, credentials or archive outputs, and does not provide a public sign-in service. The utility is not an official school application.

Google Drive access

Drive read-only permission is used to locate and download source backups. Drive file permission is used to create and update the utility's own private output files. The utility is designed to preserve original backups and exclude shared-folder publication.
